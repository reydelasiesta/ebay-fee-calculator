USPS zones aren't a fixed map of the country — each zone is the distance band between a specific ship-from ZIP and a specific destination ZIP. Enter both ZIPs above and this calculator queries USPS's own live "Get Zone for ZIP Code Pair" tool for that exact pair — the real answer, not an estimate, and it works for whatever ZIP you ship from (results are cached briefly so repeat lookups are instant). Explanations & Key

Glossary
COGS / Item cost

Cost of Goods Sold — what you originally paid to acquire the item. This is the number that goes in the "Item cost / COGS" field.

Final Value Fee (FVF)

eBay's main selling fee — a percentage of your total sale (item price + shipping charged, and yes, sales tax too), charged only when the item actually sells. The rate depends on category and your Store tier.

Insertion Fee

The fee to create a listing at all, charged whether or not it sells. Most sellers get up to 250 free per month; Store subscribers get more.

Per-Order Fee

A flat add-on to the Final Value Fee: $0.30 if the order total is $10.00 or less, $0.40 if it's over $10.00.

Store Subscription (Starter/Basic/Premium/Anchor/Enterprise)

A monthly plan that gives more free listings and, from Basic tier up, a genuinely lower Final Value Fee rate. Starter only gets the listing-allowance benefit — its fee rate is identical to having no Store at all.

Below Standard Seller Performance

eBay evaluates your account on the 20th of each month against metrics like late shipments, cancellations, and defect rate. A "Below Standard" rating adds a 6% surcharge (7% after 4+ consecutive months) to your Final Value Fees for the following calendar month.

Item Not As Described (INAD) rate

How often buyers open "item not as described" return requests against you. A "Very High" rating in a category triggers its own separate surcharge (5%, or 6% after 4+ consecutive months) — independent of the Below Standard surcharge above.

Top Rated Seller

eBay's best performance tier. Top Rated and Above Standard sellers are exempt from the Below Standard surcharge.

Promoted Listings (Standard)

Pay-per-sale advertising. You set an ad rate %, but it's only charged on sales eBay actually attributes to a promoted click — not on every sale you make.

International Fee

An extra 1.65% when the buyer or delivery address is outside the US (and you're not using eBay International Shipping). This is separate from, and in addition to, the Final Value Fee.

Currency Conversion Charge

A 3% fee eBay keeps when it has to convert your funds between currencies on a cross-border sale. Not modeled in this calculator.

Dispute Fee

A flat $20.00 fee if you're found responsible for a disputed amount (e.g., a chargeback), excluding sales tax, per dispute.

Margin vs. ROI

Margin = profit ÷ sale price — how much of what the buyer paid is profit. ROI = profit ÷ item cost — how much you made relative to what you spent to acquire the item.

eBay gotchas — need to know
  • "Free shipping" doesn't lower your fees. The Final Value Fee applies to (item price + shipping charged) either way, so offering free shipping just moves the cost into the item price — the total money changing hands, and the fee on it, stays the same.
  • You're fee'd on the sales tax too. eBay's "total amount of the sale" — the base the Final Value Fee is calculated on — includes any sales tax collected, even though that tax isn't yours; eBay collects and remits it on your behalf in most states.
  • A bad month's penalty is delayed. Below Standard status is decided on the 20th of the month, and the surcharge doesn't apply until the following calendar month — there's a lag between the behavior and the fee.
  • Listing in two categories charges two insertion fees for the same listing, and the higher of the two Final Value Fees applies if it sells.
  • Your Store subscription is a fixed monthly bill, not a per-item fee. This calculator only computes single-item math — it doesn't subtract your Store's monthly cost. Divide that cost across your monthly sales volume yourself to know its true per-item drag.
  • The per-order fee has a real cliff at $10. A $10.00 order pays $0.30; a $10.01 order pays $0.40.
  • Starter Store ≠ a fee discount. It gets you more free listings, but the Final Value Fee rate is identical to having no Store at all. The rate only drops at Basic tier and up.
  • Promoted Listings should only be toggled on for attributed sales. If you enter an ad rate for every calculation regardless of whether that specific sale came from a promoted click, you'll overstate your real fees.
